# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Welcome different experience levels, acknowledge mistakes, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not continue unwanted contact or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

The repository maintainer, [mikeholownych](https://github.com/mikeholownych), moderates project spaces and may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban accounts according to severity and recurrence. Moderation should be proportionate and protect affected people.

For public project-space misconduct, use GitHub's Report content control to notify repository administrators. Use GitHub's abuse-reporting process for private safety concerns or concerns involving the maintainer; do not post sensitive details in an issue. This project does not currently advertise a separate confidential conduct mailbox or appeal panel.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project. It does not promise investigation deadlines or confidentiality beyond what the reporting channel provides.
