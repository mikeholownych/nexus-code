Bottom line per question:

1. **Contrastive probing without ground truth: borrow it offline, skip it as an online per-turn mechanism.**
   - It needs a rerun of the same task, which is costly and has side effects in a live turn.
   - Our 2026-10-04 context research already rejected a per-turn context planner.

   - Our tuned and dogfood sets have verifiers, so a plain pass-rate comparison is stronger evidence there.
   - The useful part is diagnosis from signals Nexus already records: execution tracking, `trace.jsonl`, compaction events, tool-call recovery, and verification receipts.

2. **Eight-component taxonomy: borrow the vocabulary as a checklist, skip making components model-editable.**
   - Nexus has counterparts for six of the eight; scripts have no first-class counterpart.
   - The real gap is an editable overlay, because the operating rules, tool guidance and few-shots are compiled into TypeScript.
   - Skills are the only close one-to-one match.
   - Guardrails, subagent contracts and plugins must stay in code or operator control.

3. **Durable lessons memory: borrow the record format and admission discipline, skip automatic admission.**
