Evaluate PR-10d against the relevant system-level expectations.

Nexus must distinguish:

- Continuation.
- Repeated-work prevention.

- Provider unavailability.
- Retries.
- Timeout handling.
- Cancellation.
- Recovery and resume.

### 8. Independent review methodology

For each potential defect:

1. State the relevant requirement or invariant.
2. Identify the exact implementation behaviour.
3. Present supporting file, line, commit, or execution evidence.
4. Describe the reproducible failure path.
5. Explain the user-visible or system-level impact.
6. Classify severity.
7. Identify the responsible implementation owner.
8. Propose an acceptance criterion for remediation.

Severity:

- `P0`: Violates task correctness, authority, security, or a release-critical invariant.
- `P1`: Significant functional or integration defect.
- `P2`: Noncritical correctness gap or maintainability issue.
- `P3`: Optional enhancement or presentation improvement.

Do not elevate speculative risks to P0 without a concrete failure mechanism.

Do not dismiss confirmed requirements merely because a workaround exists.

### 9. Review disposition

Issue exactly one PR-10d disposition:

- `PLAN10_PR10D_REVIEW_ACCEPTED`
- `PLAN10_PR10D_REVIEW_ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`
- `PLAN10_PR10D_REVIEW_CHANGES_REQUIRED`
- `PLAN10_PR10D_REVIEW_BLOCKED`

#### ACCEPTED

All approved PR-10d requirements are satisfied with adequate evidence and no unresolved acceptance-blocking defects.

#### ACCEPTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS

The change meets its functional acceptance requirements, with documented nonblocking limitations.

#### CHANGES_REQUIRED

One or more confirmed requirements, correctness conditions, or critical integration invariants are unmet.

#### BLOCKED

The review cannot be completed because the authoritative scope, review snapshot, or necessary evidence is unavailable.

Do not use the overall Nexus v1.0.0 readiness disposition as a substitute for the PR-level review.
