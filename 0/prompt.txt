## Findings, ranked

**P1-A′. Composer editing keys choose a decision, including session-wide approval.** `live_state.rs:1266-1301`.
- **Observed:** once the panel is settled, `←`, `→` and `Tab` move a choice that starts on nothing and wraps around: `→` = Approve, `→→` = all later, `←` = Deny, `←←` = all later. An `Enter` straight after takes it. These are the composer's cursor keys (`live_state.rs:1557-1560`), and `Tab` is slash-command completion. They choose even when the panel says "typing ignored".
- **Unit results:** after a 700 ms pause, `Left Left Enter`, `Right Right Enter` and `Home Right Right Enter` all approved all later actions; `Right Enter` approved once.
- **PTY results:** the `cursor_keys` probe approved all later actions and switched to auto (2/2). The `tab_complete` probe (`/rev`, pause, `Tab`, `Enter`) approved once (2/2).
- **Expected:** keys a typist uses on a draft should never reach all later actions without a confirmation that is not composer muscle memory.
- **Fix:**
  - Never take all later actions from an arrow-made choice with a single `Enter`. Require a second confirm (for example `Enter` again after the "confirms approving this and every later action" hint has been drawn for at least 400 ms), or require a distinct key.
  - Once the panel has been typed into, the first arrow should only reveal the choices. An `Enter` should decide only after the shown choice has been on screen for at least 400 ms.
  - Do not wrap from the first choice to all later actions.

**P2-1. Ctrl/Alt chords are read as letter answers.** `live_state.rs:1285-1296`.
- **Observed:** `KeyCode::Char('a'|'y'|'n')` matches whatever the modifiers. Units: `Ctrl+Y` and `Alt+Y` approve, `Ctrl+N` denies, `Ctrl+A` then `Enter` 700 ms later approves all later actions. PTY: `ctrl_a_enter` approved all later actions and switched to auto (2/2).
- **Fix:** match the letters only with no modifiers, or Shift only. Treat Ctrl/Alt chords as typing (drop the choice and disarm the letters).

**P2-2. A single `y` approves when typing stopped at least 2 s before the panel appeared.** This is by design, but it is still a way to approve without having looked.

- **Who it hits:** a slow composer who pauses about 2.6 s or more and whose next word starts with y or a.
- **Fix:** also disarm the letters while the composer holds a draft (a non-empty draft means the operator is mid-composition).
