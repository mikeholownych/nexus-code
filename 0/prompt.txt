NEXUS — INDEPENDENT CURRENT-STATE REASSESSMENT AND DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION

You are performing an independent engineering reassessment of Nexus.

This is a REVIEW AND PLANNING task.

DO NOT implement fixes.
DO NOT modify Nexus source.
DO NOT merge, commit, push, publish, rebase, reset, stash, clean, or discard anything.
DO NOT alter the operator's existing uncommitted work.
DO NOT start another experiment unless explicitly authorized after this review.
DO NOT assume the previous roadmap, architecture, defect priorities, or research direction remains correct.

Your job is to determine, from current reality and accumulated evidence:

    Given what Nexus actually is today, everything we have now learned,
    and what we currently understand Nexus needs to become, what is the
    shortest coherent progression toward a workable system?

The output must be an independent assessment. Prior reports, agent conclusions,
designs, classifications, and architectural ideas are EVIDENCE INPUTS, not
authority.

========================================================================
1. OPERATING PRINCIPLES
========================================================================

Apply these principles throughout the review:

- Distrust by Default.
- Trust, but Verify.
- Evidence Changes What We Know.
- Control what you can. Constrain the authority of what you cannot control.
- Forward progress matters.
- Thinking may be graph-shaped. Execution still needs a next action.
- Implementation != demonstrated usefulness != durable architecture.
- A defect is not automatically the next task merely because it exists.
- A new idea is not automatically architecture merely because it is promising.
- A prior PASS remains historical evidence; relevant change affects its
  applicability rather than rewriting history.
- Tests are evidence about properties under tested conditions, not immutable
  product authority.
- LLM output is input to the system, not authority over the system.
- Nexus should be governable, but Nexus is not the governor.
- Nexus provides reasonable safeguards; it is not intended to become a
  governance/compliance/policy-enforcement platform.

Do not optimize for agreement with previous analyses.

Where evidence conflicts, preserve the conflict and investigate only as far as
necessary to determine whether it changes the development direction.

========================================================================
2. CURRENT PRODUCT PRIORITY
========================================================================

For this development phase, the primary objective is:

    Can Nexus complete its objective despite perceived blockers?

Current ordering:

    1. Objective completion / recovery / workflow mechanics
    2. Completion reliability
    3. Reasoning and output quality
    4. Efficiency and optimization

Shorthand:

    Finish reliably
        -> improve what was produced
        -> make producing it efficient

Accuracy, eloquence, optimal reasoning, and output quality are currently
NON-GATING for objective completion.

However, DO NOT weaken the meaning of COMPLETED.

COMPLETED means the requested deliverable actually exists.

A model saying "done", returning final prose, emitting task_completed, exhausting
a todo list, or stopping inference is only a candidate-completion signal.

Current terminal concepts:

- COMPLETED
    Requested deliverable actually exists.

- ABANDONED_BOUNDEDLY
    Nexus deliberately determines that continued attempts are no longer
    productively advancing the objective and exits in a controlled way.
    This is controlled behavior, but it is NOT objective completion.

- UNCONTROLLED_FAILURE
    Crash, hang, unrecovered context death, pathological loop until external
    intervention, lost workflow state, silent disappearance, or equivalent.
    This should trend toward essentially zero.

========================================================================
5. REASSESS THE DEFECT BACKLOG AGAINST FORWARD DEVELOPMENT
========================================================================

Do NOT create a generic "fix all defects first" plan.

For each material known defect, determine its relationship to the next coherent
development progression.

Classify it as one of:

A. MUST RESOLVE FIRST
   It prevents or materially contaminates the next development work.

B. RESOLVE AS PART OF FORWARD DEVELOPMENT
   The next capability naturally replaces or repairs the defective mechanism.

C. PRESERVE AS REPRODUCER / ACCEPTANCE CASE
   Forward development may supersede the implementation, but the demonstrated
   failure remains useful for proving the new behavior.

D. DEFER
   Real defect, but not material to the current progression.

E. SUPERSEDED
   The affected mechanism is being legitimately replaced and independent repair
   would provide little value.

F. NEEDS INVESTIGATION
   Only where uncertainty materially changes what should be done next.

Do not use "forward development" as an excuse to hide unresolved defects.

If a defect is expected to disappear through new development, require the
original reproducer or equivalent acceptance case to demonstrate that it is
actually resolved before closing it.

========================================================================
6. REASSESS THE PRODUCT MODEL
========================================================================

Evaluate, rather than assume, the following concepts.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Durable objective reference
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Investigate whether Nexus needs a durable representation of:

- original intent;
- requested deliverable;
- required coverage;
- scope and constraints;
- operator amendments/decisions;
- completion definition.

The objective should not conceptually depend on survival of an individual
inference context.

Do not assume a particular storage representation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Durable consequences of work
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Consider whether Nexus needs durable representation of:

- workflow state;
- accumulated work product;
- evidence/artifacts;
- material findings;
- failed or ruled-out approaches;
- epoch/workstream dispositions;
- dependencies and blockers.

Do NOT assume that full transcripts should remain active context.

A useful working principle is:

    Context is ephemeral.
    Intent, progress, evidence, work product, and material outcomes are durable.

Also evaluate:

    Do not make context durable.
    Make the consequences of useful context durable, and reconstruct disposable
    working context as required to keep pursuing the objective.

Summarization is a possible context-materialization technique, NOT automatically
the durability mechanism.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Situational awareness
----------------------------------------------------------------------

This is an important candidate concept.

Evaluate whether Nexus needs:

1. LOCAL SITUATIONAL AWARENESS
   What an execution epoch needs for its immediate work.

2. RELATIONAL SITUATIONAL AWARENESS
   What workstream A needs to know about B, C, defects, findings, dependencies,
   changes, assumptions, artifacts, etc. because they materially affect A.

3. GLOBAL SITUATIONAL AWARENESS
   What the objective/orchestration level needs to know about the entire
   undertaking.

Shared awareness does NOT imply shared transcript.

Consider the principle:

    Shared knowledge does not require shared attention.

For example, A may need to know:

    B is modifying interface X.
    B is blocked by D.
    B produced artifact Y required by A.
    B invalidated assumption Z.

A probably does not need B's entire reasoning history unless deeper inspection
becomes material.

Evaluate:

    Durable global understanding
        +
    selectively materialized local awareness

as a concept, not as a predetermined implementation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
D. Relational / graph concepts
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Do NOT assume Nexus needs a graph database.

Instead assess whether graph-like semantics would improve the representation of
relationships such as:

    REQUIRES
    DEPENDS_ON
    BLOCKED_BY
    CAN_COEXIST_WITH
    MUST_SERIALIZE_WITH
    MUST_REMAIN_INDEPENDENT_OF
    PRODUCES
    SATISFIES
    SUPPORTS
    CONTRADICTS
    INVALIDATES_ASSUMPTION_OF
    SUPERSEDES
    RULED_OUT_FOR
    MATERIAL_TO
    UNKNOWN_RELATIONSHIP

Inspect whether Nexus already contains machinery that provides some of this.

Consider concepts previously encountered in Graphify or similar work only as
candidate mechanisms to cannibalize where useful.

Do not adopt another project's architecture wholesale.

Ask whether a durable objective may be fundamentally relational while an
inference context is merely a temporary projection of the relevant relationship
space.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
E. Objective-level orchestration / supervision
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Determine whether the desired behavior belongs:

- inside the existing execution loop;
- as extensions to ApplicationCore/current orchestration;
- in a distinct supervisor;
- in a broader orchestrator;
- in multiple cooperating layers;
- or somewhere else.

Do not decide this before inspecting the current implementation.

The capability under consideration is broader than "keep retrying an agent."

Potential responsibilities include:

- preserve objective continuity across inference epochs;
- determine what work actually remains;
- decompose work where useful;
- identify dependencies;
- identify real blockers;
- determine what work can coexist;
- determine what must serialize;
- determine what should remain epistemically independent;
- identify the critical path;
- allocate finite inference resources;
- choose when escalation is warranted;
- accumulate work products;
- respond to discoveries that change the work graph;
- trigger completion reconciliation.

Treat a blocker relationally:

    condition X blocks required work Y

not automatically:

    condition X blocks the entire objective.

Evaluate whether:

    Blocked(A) != Blocked(Objective)

whenever other useful paths remain.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
F. Escalation and differentiated capability
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Consider the operational model:

    routine autonomous execution
        -> exception / uncertainty
        -> escalation to stronger/specialized resource
        -> useful consequence becomes durable
        -> routine autonomous execution resumes

Do not assume the strongest model should remain permanently in the critical
path.

Investigate whether durable situational knowledge could allow expensive
reasoning to resolve uncertainty once, while cheaper/faster resources execute
against what has subsequently been established.

This is a hypothesis, not an implementation requirement.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
G. Completion reconciliation
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Evaluate the concept:

    objective completion =
        registered intent reconciled against observable accumulated reality

Execution participants should not be sole completion authority.

Potential lifecycle:

    REGISTER INTENT
        -> execute / recover / delegate / investigate
        -> accumulate durable consequences
        -> CANDIDATE COMPLETION
        -> COMPLETION RECONCILIATION
        -> either:
             COMPLETE
           or
             derive unresolved delta and continue

Separate:

- completion reconciliation:
    Has the requested objective actually been satisfied?

- finalization:
    How should accumulated work be assembled into the requested deliverable?

Finalization may expose a missing requirement and return the objective to active
work.

For the CURRENT phase, keep completion semantics intentionally simple:

1. What was requested?
2. What constitutes the requested work product?
3. What coverage/data must it contain?
4. Does accumulated observable reality contain it?

Modifiers such as:

    thoughtful
    elegant
    concise
    high-quality
    optimal

belong primarily to later quality qualification unless they materially define
the existence of the requested deliverable.

Complete != correct in the current development phase.

========================================================================
8. CONTEXT AND FINITE INFERENCE RESOURCES
========================================================================

Do not treat 65K context as inherently inadequate.

Do not treat the inference host as the presumed limiting component.

The current systems question is:

    Given finite inference resources, how should Nexus orchestrate the available
    capability to maximize probability of objective completion?

Context exhaustion should be evaluated as a potential inference-epoch boundary,
not automatically objective death.

Consider:

    context exhausted
        -> preserve material consequences
        -> materialize appropriate next context
        -> continue objective

But determine from the current implementation what machinery actually exists and
what is missing.

Do not optimize tokens, cache hit rate, context residency, GPU utilization, or
parallelism merely because those metrics can be improved.

An apparent local inefficiency may protect global process success.

Examples potentially worth preserving where justified:

- independent review;
- deliberate isolation;
- redundant validation;
- durable evidence;
- deterministic reconciliation;
- bounded re-investigation;
- unused worker capacity where parallel work is inappropriate.

Measure efficiency against success of the overall process.
