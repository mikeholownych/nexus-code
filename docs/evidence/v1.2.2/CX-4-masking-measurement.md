# CX-4: tool-output masking, measured (2026-11-21)

**Result:** with `NEXUS_TOOL_OUTPUT_MASK_TOKENS=2000`, every run still passed and the prompt tokens sent to the model roughly
halved. **Decision for v1.2.2: masking stays opt-in; 2000 is the documented recommended value.** A default is deferred until
it is measured on more than one model (see "Why not a default yet").

## Method

- Tasks: `cx4-verbose-fix` (a test runner printing about 1000 lines per run), `cx4-log-triage` (the cause on two lines of a
  6000-line log), `cx4-wide-rename` (a helper renamed across 18 files). Each has a hidden behavioural check and a regression
  command; they are generated by `eval/gen_cx4_tasks.py` and are not part of core-v1.
- Arms: `eval/configs/cx4-unmasked.json` and `eval/configs/cx4-masked-2000.json` (the same config plus
  `toolOutputMaskTokens: 2000`), run by `eval/run_cx4_measurement.sh`, 5 runs per task per arm, one at a time, under the
  GATEWAY claim, 05:47 to 06:41 UTC.
- Model: gateway alias `engineering/lead`, 65536 context. The same built agent binary served both arms (one executable
  SHA-256 across all 30 records).
- Per-run records: [`cx4-masking-measurement-2026-11-21.json`](cx4-masking-measurement-2026-11-21.json).

## Observed

| Task | Arm | Hidden check | Regression | Prompt tokens, median (min to max) | Wall seconds, median (min to max) | Steps, median |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `cx4-log-triage` | unmasked | 5/5 | 5/5 | 229,883 (144,013 to 289,251) | 124 (94 to 152) | 11 |
| `cx4-log-triage` | masked 2000 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 133,214 (107,778 to 174,506) | 77 (62 to 91) | 11 |
| `cx4-verbose-fix` | unmasked | 5/5 | 5/5 | 183,937 (179,987 to 279,477) | 63 (53 to 204) | 7 |
| `cx4-verbose-fix` | masked 2000 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 84,294 (76,549 to 94,488) | 58 (58 to 62) | 8 |
| `cx4-wide-rename` | unmasked | 5/5 | 5/5 | 230,357 (111,197 to 282,308) | 61 (34 to 563) | 9 |
| `cx4-wide-rename` | masked 2000 | 5/5 | 5/5 | 90,866 (74,298 to 234,708) | 51 (39 to 107) | 8 |

Median prompt tokens fell by 42%, 54% and 61%. No run timed out in either arm. Median step counts moved by at most one (7 to 8 on
`cx4-verbose-fix`, 9 to 8 on `cx4-wide-rename`), so the model did not visibly pay for masking with extra re-reads on these tasks.

## What this does and does not show

- It shows no correctness cost and a large input-token saving on three large-output tasks, on one model, at one threshold.
- It does not show safety in general. All 30 runs passed, so the tasks did not discriminate: they cannot reveal a task on
  which masking loses needed evidence. Five runs per cell cannot bound a rare failure.
- Wall time is a weak signal here: the two long unmasked `cx4-wide-rename` runs (414 s and 563 s) are outliers that may be
  gateway load.
- The harness reported every run as `UNVERIFIED` or `NOT_ESTABLISHED` (the agent finished without running its own
  verification) in both arms; pass and fail above are the hidden and regression checks, not the agent's claim.
- The two arms ran back to back, not interleaved, so a drift in gateway behaviour during the hour would be confounded with
  the arm.

## Why not a default yet

The gateway serves two distinct models (`docs/evidence/v1.2.2/WM-3-gateway-aliases.md`) and only one was measured; other
thresholds were not tried; and the tasks all passed. Turning masking on by default changes what every user's model sees, so
it needs a measurement that could have failed: `engineering/deep`, at least one harder task where an early output must be
recalled verbatim late in the run, and interleaved arms. Until then the feature is opt-in, with 2000 as the value that has
evidence behind it.
