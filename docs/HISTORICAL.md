# Historical requirement and qualification records

The documents below record the Issue #12 requirement programme (Phases 1–4, work packages WP01–WP11 and their qualifications). They are **historical**. Current requirements, statuses and release gates are in [v1.0.0/REQUIREMENTS.md](v1.0.0/REQUIREMENTS.md), which carries their surviving commitments forward and records a disposition for every superseded or open item (§2).

These files are not edited, not even to add a banner, because later documents pin many of them by SHA-256. Any edit would break that provenance chain. Statuses inside them ("DEMONSTRATED", "SATISFIED") describe the time they were written. Several were later contradicted (see `v1.0.0/AUDIT.md` HIST-02).

Older reviews in `reviews/` carry an in-file banner instead, because nothing pins them by hash.
